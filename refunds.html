Refund and Cancellation Policy Order Modifications and Refunds Due to the fast-paced nature of the canteen and food preparation, our cancellation policy is strictly defined: • Cancellations: Orders cannot be canceled or modified once the payment is successful and the food preparation has started. • Out of Stock Items: If you pay for an item that is later found to be out of stock at the counter, you will be offered a replacement item of equal value or a full refund for that specific item. • Refund Processing: Approved refunds will be credited back to your original payment method (UPI/Bank Account) within 5 to 7 business days, depending on the bank's processing time.
