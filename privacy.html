Privacy Policy

At Twist & Toast, your privacy is important to us. This policy outlines how we collect and use your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect basic information required to process your food order, specifically your Name and Phone Number.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for identifying your order, displaying it on our kitchen display system, and contacting you if there is an issue with your payment or food preparation.

3. Data Protection

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. Your order history is maintained securely in our internal database for accounting purposes.
