2. Privacy Policy Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Twist & Toast collects, uses, and protects your information when you use twisttoast.in. • Information We Collect: When you place an order, we collect your name, phone number, and order details to generate your token and process your request. • Payment Information: All online payments are processed securely through our authorized banking partner/payment gateway.
